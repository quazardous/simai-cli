# tic-tac-boom — the bomb that does not go off in your face.
# Lines are typed as a person would type them; indented text is what
# the agent answers, and `think` is how long it pretends to consider it.

light the fuse
    think 2
    Forty-five seconds. Traditionally this is the part where everyone
    stares at the terminal and holds their breath.

    I would rather not.

/run sleep 45; echo BOOM

so it is still ticking?
    think 1
    It is. Under an id, somewhere else, entirely on its own time.
    We have thirty-five seconds and absolutely nothing to do with them.

/say hello from a completely unexploded terminal

/ps

and when it goes off?
    think 2
    It says BOOM into its own log, and jbx mentions it on the way past.
    Nobody was waiting for it. That is the whole trick.

/exit
